I am a project manager and applied researcher with a PhD in psychology from the University of Geneva. I design,
deploy and evaluate digital interventions for mental health: I co-designed a therapeutic video game and led the
implementation of its international randomised controlled trial across Switzerland, Israel and the United States,
working hand in hand with researchers, clinicians and engineers.

My toolbox combines project management, user-centred design, rigorous evaluation and AI-assisted methods. I enjoy
sharing research with every audience, from scientific conferences to science programmes on ARTE and France 4.
Before research, I was a professional League of Legends esports player and won the GIRLGAMER World
Championship in 2020.
