{{< cols >}}
{{< col title="The problem" >}}
A research lab had built a low-cost tangible prototype for children with little or no vision, but it had never been
studied in the classrooms it was meant for.
{{< /col >}}
{{< col title="What we did" >}}
Observed and interviewed specialised teachers, benchmarked eight tactile devices, ran demonstration workshops with
the professionals, and checked every idea against the engineers' low-cost constraint.
{{< /col >}}
{{< col title="What came out" >}}
13 prioritised recommendations, a functional specification and two mock-ups for version 2, including five safety and
legibility fixes rated as blocking.
{{< /col >}}
